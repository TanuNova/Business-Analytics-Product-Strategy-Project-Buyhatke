# Business-Analytics-Product-Strategy-Project-Buyhatke
This project was completed as part of a Business Analyst Intern assignment for Buyhatke. The objective was to apply data analytics and strategic thinking to solve real business problems using structured frameworks and insights.

Part 1: Data Analysis – Audio Sales Dataset

Objective: Analyze pricing, demand, and platform performance across audio products.

Methodology:
Cleaned raw data (handled missing values, duplicates, formatting issues).
Created derived fields such as price bands, weekday, and month.
Used Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP, and charts to summarize and visualize trends.

Key Insights:
Budget audio products (<₹2,000) dominate demand, indicating price-sensitive consumers.
Flipkart leads in volume for affordable products, while Amazon shows premium positioning.
A small number of brands contribute disproportionately to total listings, highlighting market concentration.
Clear synergy observed between platform strategy, price bands, and brand performance.

Part 2: Product Strategy – Cab Compare Feature

Problem Identified:
Low feature visibility and awareness
Weak repeat usage due to lack of personalization
Limited association of Buyhatke with travel savings

Approach:
Conducted SWOT analysis to evaluate internal and external factors.
Applied STP framework to define target users and positioning.
Designed a phased execution roadmap focusing on visibility, personalization, incentives, and partnerships.

Proposed Impact:
Improved feature discovery and engagement
Higher retention through alerts, rewards, and saved routes
Stronger brand positioning as a comprehensive smart savings platform

Tools & Skills Used
Excel / Google Sheets
Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP, Conditional Formatting
Business Analysis & Insight Generation
Product Strategy, SWOT, STP, KPI Design

Key Takeaway: This project demonstrates my ability to translate data into actionable business insights and design growth strategies by creating synergy between user behavior, platform features, and business goals.
